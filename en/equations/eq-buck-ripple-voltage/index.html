Output voltage ripple of the buck

The two parts of the buck's output ripple: the capacitor's and its ESR's.

ΔvC=ΔiL8 C fs,ΔvESR=RESR ΔiL\Delta v_C = \frac{\Delta i_L}{8\,C\,f_s}, \qquad \Delta v_{\mathrm{ESR}} = R_{\mathrm{ESR}}\,\Delta i_LΔvC​=8Cfs​ΔiL​​,ΔvESR​=RESR​ΔiL​

ΔvC\Delta v_CΔvC​ is the peak-to-peak ripple on the capacitance CCC, which absorbs the AC part of iLi_LiL​; ΔvESR\Delta v_{\mathrm{ESR}}ΔvESR​ is the drop on the capacitor's series resistance. The two are out of phase, so the total ripple lies between the larger one and their sum.
