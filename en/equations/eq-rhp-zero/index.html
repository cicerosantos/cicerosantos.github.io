Right-half-plane zero of the boost

The frequency of the boost's right-half-plane zero falls with the square of D' and with the load current.

ωRHP=R D′2−RlL≈R D′2L\omega_{\mathrm{RHP}} = \frac{R\,D'^2 - R_l}{L} \approx \frac{R\,D'^2}{L}ωRHP​=LRD′2−Rl​​≈LRD′2​

RRR is the load, D′=1−DD' = 1 - DD′=1−D, LLL the inductance and RlR_lRl​ the series resistance of the inductor and one switch. The approximation neglects the losses; the zero comes down when the load rises (smaller RRR) or the input falls (smaller D′D'D′).
