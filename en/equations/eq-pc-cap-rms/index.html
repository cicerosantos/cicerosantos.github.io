RMS current in the buck's output capacitor

The RMS current in the buck's output capacitor in CCM, which is the triangular part of the inductor current.

IC,rms=ΔiL12I_{C,\mathrm{rms}} = \frac{\Delta i_L}{\sqrt{12}}IC,rms​=12​ΔiL​​

ΔiL\Delta i_LΔiL​ is the peak-to-peak ripple of the inductor current. It holds for a triangular wave of any duty ratio, assuming a constant load current.
