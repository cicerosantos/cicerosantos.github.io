RC and RL circuits: from the circuit to the pole-zero form

How to bring a passive circuit with one capacitor or one inductor to the factored form, through the gains at DC and at high frequency and the resistance the component sees, with the circuits grouped by the form they produce.

First, see: First-order pole and zero, Inverted forms and the sign on the Bode plot
