    Concepts

    • Compensating the ×10 probeThe passive ×10 probe as two R∥C in series, with the resistive divider at DC and the capacitive one at an edge; R1C1 = R2C2 puts the zero on the pole, and a square wave shows whether the probe is overcompensated or undercompensated.
    • Sallen-Key active filters: the pole pair as a circuitAn op-amp as a follower, two resistors and two capacitors give the pole pair with f₀ and Q set by the components; the low-pass, the high-pass in the inverted pair's form, the design equations and the sections of a Butterworth cascade.
