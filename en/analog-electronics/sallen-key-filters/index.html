Sallen-Key active filters: the pole pair as a circuit

An op-amp as a follower, two resistors and two capacitors give the pole pair with f₀ and Q set by the components; the low-pass, the high-pass in the inverted pair's form, the design equations and the sections of a Butterworth cascade.

First, see: Butterworth: the Q of each section and the order n, Second-order forms: zeros, undamped, inverted and right-half-plane, The op-amp: feedback through impedances

In practice

A real op-amp has a finite bandwidth: in the high-pass, the passband ends there; in the low-pass, above it, the signal leaks through C1C_1C1​ to the output, whose impedance rises with frequency, and the attenuation stops growing. Choose an op-amp with a bandwidth well above f0f_0f0​, even more so with a high QQQ. QQQ depends on a ratio of components: with 5% capacitors, C1/C2C_1/C_2C1​/C2​ may be off by 10%, and QQQ by about 5%.

Exercise

Three-way crossover. An active loudspeaker splits the signal into bass, midrange and treble outputs, with crossovers at 120 Hz and 1.44 kHz, using fourth-order Linkwitz-Riley filters: each filter is two identical second-order Butterworth Sallen-Key stages in cascade, −6 dB at the corner. Bass: a low-pass at 120 Hz; midrange: a high-pass at 120 Hz times a low-pass at 1.44 kHz; treble: a high-pass at 1.44 kHz. With C2C_2C2​ = 47 nF (C1C_1C1​ = 2 × 47 nF in parallel) in the 120 Hz low-pass, 4.7 nF in the 1.44 kHz one, CCC = 100 nF in the 120 Hz high-pass and 10 nF in the 1.44 kHz one, compute the resistors and sketch the midrange asymptotes. Why not a single second-order Butterworth Sallen-Key in each output?

Answer
  1. Low-pass: R=1/(2 ω0C2)R = 1/(\sqrt 2\,\omega_0C_2)R=1/(2​ω0​C2​): 19.95 kΩ at 120 Hz and 16.63 kΩ at 1.44 kHz.
  2. High-pass: R1=1/(2 ω0C)R_1 = 1/(\sqrt 2\,\omega_0C)R1​=1/(2​ω0​C) and R2=2R1R_2 = 2R_1R2​=2R1​: 9.38 and 18.76 kΩ at 120 Hz; 7.82 and 15.63 kΩ at 1.44 kHz.
  3. Midrange asymptotes: +80 dB per decade up to 120 Hz, 0 dB up to 1.44 kHz and −80 dB per decade above; the two corners are more than a decade apart, and they barely interact.
