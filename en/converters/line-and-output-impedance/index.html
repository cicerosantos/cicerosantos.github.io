The measurement in QSPICE

The ports are measured like the loop gain in Measuring the loop gain by injecting into the duty. The simulated firmware block (scenario.cpp) adds a small sine to the input voltage, for GvgG_{vg}Gvg​, or to the load's conductance, for ZoutZ_{out}Zout​: a current V g sin⁡ωtV\,g\,\sin\omega tVgsinωt in parallel with the load. Every period, at the ADC sample's instant, it records the output, not quantized, and the input or the injected current. The ratio of the Fourier components of the two, with a Hann window over 40 cycles after 30 ms, is the transfer function.

In open loop, the duty is held at the model's steady-state value (in the buck, the lossless Vo/VinV_o/V_{in}Vo​/Vin​), plus the dead time's share: G1 turns on only a dead time after the start of the period. In closed loop, the 2p2z runs, and the model is the open-loop one divided by 1+T1 + T1+T (equation Sensitivity), at the run's mean duty. Every point was measured again at half the amplitude, and none changed by more than 0.17 dB and 0.9°: the response is linear.

The model compared is the switched circuit sampled at the ADC's instant, like that of Sampling instant and the ESR in the boost, with the duty fixed. At low frequency it is the averaged model above. In the buck the two coincide; in the boost, the averaged one lags the phase of GvgG_{vg}Gvg​ by 2° to 7° from 1 to 5 kHz, and QSPICE sides with the sampled one.
