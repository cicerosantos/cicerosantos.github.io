Digital control of DC-DC converters

A course on designing the digital control of switched DC-DC converters, from the plant model to the interrupt code, always with the lab's three converters: the buck, the boost and the four-switch buck-boost. The numbers come from QSPICE simulations. The notes are grouped in collections, and the trails walk through them in order.

    Where to start

    • I know classical controlCheck what you know in a few minutes with the diagnostic (Start here), or review reading Bode plots in the From Laplace to Bode trail.
    • New to controlStart with the diagnostic (Start here): each answer says which note to read, and the From Laplace to Bode and Closing and compensating the loop trails take you all the way.
    • Going to hardwareCheck the basics with the diagnostic (Start here); then follow the From the analog buck to the digital loop and From the boost to the digital loop trails, up to the designed and measured loop, and then From compensator to firmware.

    Full path: From Laplace to Bode → Closing and compensating the loop → From the analog buck to the digital loop → From the boost to the digital loop → From compensator to firmware. Detours: From circuit to transfer function, Buck power stage.
