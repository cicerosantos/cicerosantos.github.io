Read the plant

First unit of classical control, in order: from the transfer function to the factored form, the second-order filters and the asymptotic Bode plot of the buck's plant.

This trail is the first unit of classical control: read a converter's plant as a transfer function, in factored form, and draw its Bode plot with asymptotes. Each step uses the numbers of the lab's converters. If you do not know where to start, first answer the questions in Start here.

  1. Transfer function and factored form: the buck's plant as a ratio of polynomials, with the DC gain, the poles and the zeros in factored form.
  2. First-order pole and zero: the simplest block, a real pole or zero, with its asymptotes and the 3 dB error at the corner.
  3. Second-order pole pair and quality factor Q: two poles together can oscillate, and the QQQ factor says how much; it is the buck's LC.
  4. Second-order filters: the catalogue: the five second-order filters, from low-pass to all-pass, recognised by their asymptote.
  5. Bode by composition: from factored form to asymptote: add the asymptotes of each factor to draw the plant's Bode plot, and read the equation back from the curve.
  6. From circuit to factored form (reinforcement): optional reinforcement, to see where the plant's formulas come from, starting from the circuit.

End of the trail

From here, go back to the converters: the From the analog buck to the digital loop trail takes the buck from the power stage to the firmware, and the From the boost to the digital loop shows what changes when the plant has a zero in the right half-plane. The next units of classical control, on the loop, the margins and the compensators, will join this trail when they are ready.
