This course says loop gain and open/closed loop (malha aberta/fechada, in Portuguese). In the power-electronics literature the same TTT also appears as an open-loop transfer function (função de transferência de laço aberto, FTLA). "Open-loop gain" can mean only the gain KKK of a compensator, not the whole TTT.
