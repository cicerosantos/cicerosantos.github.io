In the Brazilian power-electronics literature, the same TTT can also be found as função de transferência de laço aberto (FTLA, open-loop transfer function) or função de transferência de malha aberta compensada. The Portuguese notes say ganho de malha (loop gain) and malha aberta/fechada (open/closed loop), as Brazilian control courses do. Mind "open-loop gain": in some control textbooks it is only the gain KKK, not the whole TTT.

Three symbols that change meaning

  • LLL there is the loop gain; here it is the inductance, and the loop gain is TTT.
  • TTT there is the complementary sensitivity; here TsT_sTs​ is the switching and sampling period, and TTT the loop gain.
  • β\betaβ there is the sensor's divider and, in another lecture, the lag's factor; here β\betaβ is the sensor's feedback, the same divider, and it is 1 in the lab's converters.
