First, see: Bode by composition: from factored form to asymptote

Six exercises to practice reading back, as in Bode by composition: from factored form to asymptote, from the simplest to the hardest. In each one, write G(s)G(s)G(s) on paper, with the gain, the corners and each factor's form, and only then open the answer. Under each plot, the figure lists the corners and, separately, the 0 dB frequency of each integrator or differentiator; a pair's QQQ is read on the exact curve, which the figure's switch turns on. The "Show the answer" button gives the equation; the answer box, the reasoning. In the answers, the magnitude is in dB per decade, and the phase comes with the intervals of its lines.
