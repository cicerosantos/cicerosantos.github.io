Butterworth: the Q of each section and the order n

The maximally flat filter, built as a cascade of pole pairs with the same f₀ and a real pole for odd orders, with the table of Q from n = 2 to 8 and a band-pass by composition.

In the converters

The buck's ideal lossless LC pair would only be Butterworth with RC/L=0.707R\sqrt{C/L} = 0.707RC/L​=0.707: with 22 µH and 47 µF, a 0.48 Ω load, 19 A at 9 V. The plant is never flat, and it is the loop that damps it. The measurement filter in front of the ADC, on the other hand, is a design choice, and in its active version it is a Butterworth with n=2n = 2n=2 (Sensing and anti-aliasing filter).
