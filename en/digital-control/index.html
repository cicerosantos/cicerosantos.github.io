    How-to

    • Anti-windup and duty saturationWhen the duty hits its limit, the integrator must not keep accumulating error: the integrator clamp, the 2p2z velocity form and back-calculation.
    • Derivative kick and derivative on the measurementOn a reference step, a derivative term computed on the error jumps in a single period and saturates the duty; differentiating the measurement takes the reference out of that term.
    • Interrupt deadline and budgetThe control routine must write the CCR before the update event that copies it; the budget is the period minus the trigger, conversion, entry and computation, and only a measurement with a pin or the DWT, on production code, says whether it fits.
    • Measuring the loop gain by injecting into the dutyA sine added to the duty in the firmware gives the loop gain as −C/U-C/U−C/U; the amplitude has to grow with frequency so the ADC sample moves several codes, and a second measurement at half the amplitude shows whether the point is linear.
    • Mini-project: the firmware of one converterWrite and check in C the digital loop of the boost or the buck-boost, with a checklist, two calculations with answers, and a delivery sheet with the expected values.
    • Robust design across the operating rangeOne fixed compensator for a plant that changes with the input and the load: the corners of the range, the crossover between about 1/5 and 1/3 of the right-half-plane zero in the worst case, and the margins checked at every point.
    • Soft-start and pre-biasingThe reference ramp, which avoids saturation during the transition, and pre-biasing, where the output already has voltage before the converter starts switching.
