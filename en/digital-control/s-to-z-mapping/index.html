Poles, not zeros

The zeros do not follow this rule: they depend on when the input acts within the period, and zeros that G(s)G(s)G(s) does not have can appear. The buck's sampled model has a zero at z=−0.483z = -0.483z=−0.483: it is the sampling zero, set by the edge that acts at DTsDT_sDTs​ (without the ESR, near −D/(1−D)=−0.6-D/(1-D) = -0.6−D/(1−D)=−0.6; with a plain ZOH, near −1-1−1 without the ESR and −0.82-0.82−0.82 with it). Why everything crowds near z=1z = 1z=1

In the buck, the LC resonance and the crossover lie far below fsf_sfs​; with ∣pTs∣≪1|pT_s| \ll 1∣pTs​∣≪1, z=epTs≈1+pTsz = e^{pT_s} \approx 1 + pT_sz=epTs​≈1+pTs​: what matters up to the crossover fits in a disc of radius 2πfcTs=0.252\pi f_c T_s = 0.252πfc​Ts​=0.25 around z=1z = 1z=1. That is why the z-plane figure of The 2p2z compensator has a "near z = 1" view.

The price is in the coefficients: the roots depend on small differences between them. The 2p2z's double zeros, at 0.9391, come from the full coefficients; with the four digits printed in The 2p2z compensator, which comes later in the trail (0.23110.23110.2311, −0.4341-0.4341−0.4341, 0.20380.20380.2038), the discriminant goes from 0 to 5×10−55 \times 10^{-5}5×10−5, and the roots split into 0.95450.95450.9545 and 0.92390.92390.9239. Near a double root, the shift grows with the square root of the error. The Bode plot at the crossover barely changes (0.03 dB and 0.1°), but the integrator cannot tolerate even that: in Q15 fixed point, a1a_1a1​ and a2a_2a2​ in different formats push it to z=1.000036z = 1.000036z=1.000036, outside the circle.

Tustin, an approximation of the exact map

The controller needs a ratio of polynomials in zzz, and s=ln⁡(z)/Tss = \ln(z)/T_ss=ln(z)/Ts​ is not one. Tustin replaces esTse^{sT_s}esTs​ by the (1,1) Padé approximant, (1+sTs/2)/(1−sTs/2)(1 + sT_s/2)/(1 - sT_s/2)(1+sTs​/2)/(1−sTs​/2): it takes the whole jωj\omegajω axis onto the circle, instead of only the strip up to π/Ts\pi/T_sπ/Ts​, and so it warps the frequencies (The bilinear (Tustin) transform and prewarping).
