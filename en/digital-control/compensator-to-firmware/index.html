From compensator to firmware

From the designed 2p2z to the board's code: the PWM timer, the ADC trigger, the interrupt deadline, the arithmetic, saturation, soft-start and the mini-project.

Start the trail →

This trail turns the designed compensator into firmware that works on a board, also outside steady state: at start-up, in saturation and on reference steps. It starts from the loop designed and measured in the From the analog buck to the digital loop and From the boost to the digital loop trails.

  1. PWM timer and shadow registers: the timer, the CCR and the preload: what the duty cycle becomes on the board, with edge, center, dead time and minimum pulse.
  2. Centered PWM on the H7: case B: optional the centered pulse from two comparators of the H7's up-counter, with a delay of 1.5 Ts1.5\,T_s1.5Ts​ to the pulse center, whatever DDD.
  3. ADC trigger and sample instant: from the timer's trigger to the instant the ADC actually samples, and where to put that instant in the period; in the boost, ahead of G1's edge.
  4. Interrupt deadline and budget: the routine must write the CCR before the update event, and only a measurement, with a pin or the DWT, says whether the budget fits.
  5. Floating point or fixed point: floating point or fixed point, a design choice with a cost in cycles.
  6. Q15 fixed point: in fixed point, the 2p2z's coefficients get a scale and a Q format.
  7. Anti-windup and duty saturation: when the duty cycle saturates, the controller must stop accumulating error.
  8. Derivative kick and derivative on the measurement: on a reference step, the derivative of the error jumps; the derivative of the measurement does not.
  9. The 2p2z in C, in the interrupt: everything together, in the interrupt's C code.
  10. Soft-start and pre-biasing: the reference ramp, which avoids saturation at start-up, and an output that already has voltage before the converter starts switching.
  11. Mini-project: the firmware of one converter: write and check the firmware of the boost or the buck-boost, with the note's checklist.
