Computation and PWM delay

From the ADC sample to the PWM edge it moves, (1+D) Ts(1 + D)\,T_s(1+D)Ts​ go by in this firmware, and this delay costs 360∘ f (1+D) Ts360^\circ\, f\,(1 + D)\,T_s360∘f(1+D)Ts​ of phase at the crossover.

First, see: Loop gain, crossover frequency and margins, Sampling and the zero-order hold (ZOH)

In an analog loop, a change at the output reaches the PWM comparator almost at once. In a digital one, the information travels a chain: the ADC converts, the interrupt computes, the PWM register is updated and, only then, an edge of the pulse moves. Each stage delays the response, and a pure delay tdt_dtd​ is e−stde^{-s t_d}e−std​: it leaves the magnitude of the loop gain alone, but takes phase.

The chain in this site's firmware

Period kkk starts at t=kTst = kT_st=kTs​:

  1. Sample and conversion. The timer triggers the ADC at kTskT_skTs​ (Sampling and the zero-order hold (ZOH)). The conversion takes from hundreds of nanoseconds to a few microseconds.
  2. Computation. The end-of-conversion interrupt runs the PID or the 2p2z and writes the new duty to the compare register (CCR).
  3. Update in the next period. With preload on, the new CCR only takes effect at the next update event, at (k+1)Ts(k+1)T_s(k+1)Ts​. This guarantees a whole pulse with the old value, even if the computation runs late, and costs one period.
  4. The modulated edge. In a trailing-edge (sawtooth) PWM, the switch turns on at the start of the period and off at (k+1+D)Ts(k+1+D)T_s(k+1+D)Ts​. Only that edge depends on ddd; the duty perturbation enters the plant there, DTsDT_sDTs​ after the start.

Other timings

  • Update in the same period. Without preload, if the new CCR is written before the falling edge of the current pulse, the delay drops to DTsDT_sDTs​. The computation must fit in DTsDT_sDTs​: in the buck, 0.375×5 µs=1.9 µs0.375 \times 5\ \text{µs} = 1.9\ \text{µs}0.375×5 µs=1.9 µs, conversion included.
  • Center-aligned PWM. The counter counts up and down, and the pulse is centered on the valley. With the sample at the valley and the load at the peak, the effective delay, from the sample to the center of the pulse it governs, is exactly TsT_sTs​, for any DDD; both edges move, and the sample at the center of the pulse is far from them.
  • Computation that misses its deadline. If the interrupt ends after the update event, the value only takes effect one period later: (2+D)Ts(2+D)T_s(2+D)Ts​.

Jitter and priorities

The delay is only (1+D)Ts(1+D)T_s(1+D)Ts​ if the control interrupt never misses its deadline. A higher-priority communication interrupt, a slow flash access or a debug printf can push the CCR write past the update event in some periods, and the loop then has a varying delay. Give the control the highest priority, and time the routine with an output pin and the oscilloscope.

Phase budget

Before choosing fcf_cfc​, compute the phase the delay will take. If it is more than about 30°, the crossover is too high for the chosen timing.
